# Vision

Where the flagship repository's vision describes where CeloHT is headed
as a community initiative, this document describes what that means for
the dApp specifically: a single, trustworthy application where a
community member can learn, get verified as an agent, and support
reforestation — without ever having to trust CeloHT with their funds or
their keys.

That means:

- Every state-changing action happens through the user's own wallet
  signature, never a CeloHT-controlled account.
- Every number shown in the UI is either read live from a public,
  auditable contract or clearly labeled as sample data — never presented
  as real when it isn't.
- The codebase itself is the documentation of what CeloHT can and cannot
  do with a user's data or funds — not a claim made separately from the
  code.

See the flagship repository's
[`docs/vision.md`](https://github.com/celo-ht/celoht/blob/main/docs/vision.md)
for the ecosystem-wide vision.
