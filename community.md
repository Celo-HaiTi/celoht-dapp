# Community and Governance

CeloHTGovernance is deployed on Celo Sepolia and uses one-wallet-one-vote with a contract-defined USDm participation fee. This static frontend does not have a proposal indexer, so it does not present local sample proposals as live governance or submit votes against invented IDs.

Agent profiles, identity verification, and community records are off-chain concerns. They require authenticated backend infrastructure and are unavailable until configured.
